1. Introduction

RWAXI respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect through rwaxi.com, how we may use and disclose that information, and the choices available to visitors.

This policy applies to information collected through this website and related communications with RWAXI. It does not govern the privacy practices of third-party websites, platforms or services that may be linked from this website.

2. Information You Provide to Us

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you communicate with RWAXI, including information such as your name, email address, telephone number, company or organization, professional information and the contents of your communications.

If you request a demo through the form on this website, the information you include in your request contains your WhatsApp number. We will use it, together with your email address, to contact you and coordinate the scheduling of the virtual demo meeting. WhatsApp communications take place through that third-party platform and are subject to its own terms and privacy policy.

The “Request a Demo” form does not send data to this website’s servers: it prepares a message in your own email program, and the information only reaches RWAXI if you choose to send that message.

Please do not submit confidential investment account information, passwords, private keys, seed phrases, government identification numbers or other sensitive financial credentials through general website communications or email.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you access the website, certain technical information may be transmitted automatically by your browser, device, hosting provider or security infrastructure. This may include IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, pages visited, timestamps and similar technical or diagnostic information.

RWAXI does not currently use this website to intentionally collect sensitive financial credentials or blockchain private keys.

If RWAXI later implements analytics, advertising technologies, cookies or similar tracking technologies beyond those necessary for website operation and security, this Privacy Policy and any required consent mechanisms will be updated as appropriate.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through the website to:

  • respond to inquiries and communications;
  • communicate regarding RWAXI's business, services and activities;
  • evaluate potential business relationships and strategic opportunities;
  • operate, maintain, secure and improve the website;
  • detect, investigate and prevent fraud, misuse or security incidents;
  • maintain appropriate business and compliance records;
  • comply with applicable legal, regulatory or governmental requirements; and
  • protect the rights, property and security of RWAXI and others.

5. Disclosure of Information

RWAXI does not sell personal information collected through this website.

We may disclose information to service providers that support website hosting, email, security, information technology, professional services or other legitimate business operations, subject to appropriate contractual or confidentiality arrangements where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process or governmental request, or to protect the rights, security or property of RWAXI or others.

In connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, acquisition, sale of assets or similar corporate transaction, information may be transferred as part of the transaction subject to applicable law.

6. Third-Party Websites and Platforms

This website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms, including LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and Substack.

RWAXI does not control the privacy, security or data practices of those third parties. Visitors should review the privacy policies and terms applicable to any third-party service they choose to access.

7. Data Retention

RWAXI retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including legitimate business, legal, accounting, compliance and security purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the information and the context in which it was collected.

8. Data Security

RWAXI uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information under its control.

However, no Internet transmission, electronic storage system or security measure can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should use appropriate care when transmitting information electronically.

9. International Visitors and Data Transfers

RWAXI's activities may involve communications, service providers and business relationships in multiple jurisdictions.

Information may therefore be processed or stored in jurisdictions other than the jurisdiction from which a visitor accesses the website. Where required by applicable law, RWAXI will take appropriate measures regarding international transfers of personal information.

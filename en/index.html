03 / Line A · Structuring

From a project to an investment opportunity.

We work with construction companies, developers, owners and businesses on the legal, financial and documentary organization of their project, so it can be presented to investors or lenders with a clear structure.

  1. 01
    Assessment

    Review of the project, ownership of the property or asset, its stage and its capital needs.

  2. 02
    Investment structure

    Definition of the company or investment vehicle, investor rights and governance rules, together with the project's advisers.

  3. 03
    Opportunity preparation

    Organization of the information and materials needed to present the project rigorously.

  4. 04
    Access to capital

    A strategy to seek investment or financing, based on the project's profile and applicable regulations.

Tokenization, meaning the digital representation of investment shares or rights, is incorporated only when it adds value to the project and is legally viable.

04 / Line B · Technology

Technology to tokenize real estate assets.

We develop tokenization technology for real estate projects and support construction companies, developers and promoters in implementing it, with solutions tailored to the legal structure and needs of each project. Technology opens up new investment and financing possibilities; it does not guarantee results on its own.

01

Digital representation

The property, its units and the rights they grant, represented in digital form.

02

Traceable record

Transactions are recorded on blockchain, a shared digital ledger that keeps their history traceable.

03

Investor management

Tracking of holdings, economic rights and investor information.

04

Feasibility assessment

Before implementing, we assess whether tokenization is viable and suitable for the project.

Some functionality is under development. The technology scope is defined with each project.

05 / How we work

From your project to sources of capital.

Every project starts with an assessment of its viability. From there, we move forward in stages.

01

Legal

Rights, ownership and investment structures are established first.

02

Financial

The project is organized in an investment vehicle with clear rules on governance, economics and transparency.

03

Technology

Where it makes sense, blockchain technology adds traceability and allows investment rights to be represented and managed digitally.

04

Capital

The structured project can be presented to appropriate investors and sources of capital, in accordance with applicable regulations.
